## How to read a case

Each `cases/CASE-XXX-slug/` folder contains:

- **observation.md** — the author's first-hand observation + independent
  WoW-community evidence that the phenomenon is real and widespread.
- **corporate-parallel.md** — the matching corporate/organizational dynamic,
  backed by management research and reporting.
- **synthesis.md** — the parallel stated plainly, the shared mechanism, the
  disanalogies, and the strength rating.
- **sources.md** — consolidated citations for the case.

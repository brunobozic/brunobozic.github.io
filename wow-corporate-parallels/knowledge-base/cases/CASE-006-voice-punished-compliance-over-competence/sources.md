## Notes

- **C9 (Burris 2012)**, **C8 (John et al. 2019)**, and **C3 (Milliken et al. 2003)** are
  the three most on-point anchors — lead with these.
- **X1 (Judge et al.)** is the sharp reconciliation: it's not "don't be difficult," it's
  "don't name *our* problems" (self-promoting disagreeableness upward is rewarded;
  challenging the org's own practices is punished).
- `[DOI verify]`: C7 (Rosen & Tesser), C11 (Harvey). C14 (DARVO) applied by analogy —
  label as conceptual transfer, not a validated org-behavior finding.
- **W14** is first-hand (no URL); pattern externally attested, this instance is testimony.

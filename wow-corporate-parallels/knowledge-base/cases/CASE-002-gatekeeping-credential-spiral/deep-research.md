## PART A — WoW side: status, "gaming capital," and metric-as-norm

The key intellectual gain: the game-studies literature reframes GearScore/RaiderIO
as **quantified status/credential systems**, and independently supports the "metric
becomes prescriptive" reading — while also forcing an honest correction to our
"score ≠ competence" line.

### The theory of metric-driven status
- **T.L. Taylor (2006), *Play Between Worlds*, ch. "Beyond Fun: Instrumental Play
  and Power Gamers," MIT Press** (open access). [EVIDENCE — ethnography/theory]
  https://direct.mit.edu/books/oa-monograph/2800 — "power gamers" whose
  instrumental, efficiency-first play becomes the community's normative standard of
  "playing correctly." → The **root mechanism** of bar-inflation: power-gamer norms
  get imposed on everyone.
- **Consalvo (2007), *Cheating: Gaining Advantage in Videogames*, MIT Press.**
  [EVIDENCE — theory/ethnography] https://direct.mit.edu/books/monograph/4389 —
  **"gaming capital":** mastery/knowledge (via guides, databases, scores) becomes
  social currency that sorts insiders from outsiders. → **GearScore/RaiderIO =
  quantified gaming capital**, i.e. a *credential*. This is the tightest conceptual
  bridge to the corporate credentialism side.
- **Kristine Ask (2016), "The Value of Calculations: The Coproduction of Theorycraft
  and Player Practices," *Bulletin of Science, Technology & Society*.** [EVIDENCE —
  yearlong ethnography] https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0270467617690058 —
  theorycraft doesn't just *describe* optimal play, it **stabilizes one legitimate
  style and marginalizes others.** → Supports "the metric stops being descriptive
  and becomes prescriptive."
- **Nick Yee, Daedalus Project — the Achievement motivation cluster** (~40k
  respondents). [EVIDENCE — large survey] https://nickyee.com/daedalus/ — isolates
  Advancement/Mechanics/Competition motivations. → Explains **who** builds and
  enforces gatekeeping metrics and why a number is psychologically load-bearing.

### Elitism/exclusion as a structural feature
- **Williams et al. (2006), "From Tree House to Barracks," *Games and Culture*
  1(4).** DOI 10.1177/1555412006292616. [EVIDENCE] https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1555412006292616
  Identifies **elitism and social distance** among the most common sources of member
  dissatisfaction, shaped by game mechanics. → Elitism/exclusion is structural and
  *predates* GearScore — score addons **formalize a pre-existing status dynamic.**
- **CGMagazine, "Why Are We Gatekeeping MMOs?"** [OPINION — journalism]
  https://www.cgmagonline.com/articles/editorals/why-are-we-gatekeeping-mmos/ —
  players gatekeep not just clears but "your class, race or talents just to
  participate." → Bar-inflation on multiple axes beyond gear.

### The catch-22, in the tool-makers' own words
- **Wowpedia, "Gear score" (reference).** https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gear_score
  — documents score **inflation/gaming** (stacking off-spec high-ilvl gear), dev
  **Ghostcrawler's mocking "ilvl-300 epic shirt"** response, and the metric's
  disuse once Blizzard surfaced average item level. Also concedes a **genuine
  risk-reduction function** (see complications). → One citable anchor for gaming-the-
  metric, poor-proxy, and the developer's dismissiveness.
- **Raider.IO co-founder interview (TeamLiquid)** and **Wowhead interview #380545.**
  https://www.wowhead.com/news/measuring-the-success-of-a-season-interview-with-raider-io-380545
  — the vendor concedes new players "have to start doing keys… to build up that
  score," and defines the score by **run history/completion**, not a skill test.
  → The **bootstrap catch-22 admitted by the tool's own creator**; the metric
  measures *history*, not competence.
- **GearScore developer interview (Stormstrike, 2009).**
  https://stormstrike.wordpress.com/2009/11/04/more-than-just-gearscore-an-interview-with-gear-scores-developer/
  — creator's intent vs. weaponized community use, one metric-generation earlier.
- **MMO-Champion, "Raider IO needs to go… just as bad as Gearscore was in WOTLK."**
  https://www.mmo-champion.com/threads/2562744 — the **recurrence thesis is a native
  community frame**, not just our synthesis; players themselves say gear/score
  "isn't reflective of your skill."

### The era-matched catch-22 evidence
- Recommended-gear PUG threads show leaders demanding **achievements already earned
  in the content** + gear **above what the tier drops**: MMO-Champion "Recommended
  GearScore For ICC Raiding" (https://www.mmo-champion.com/threads/2641208) and
  Warmane "GS Requirements for Raids" (https://forum.warmane.com/showthread.php?t=430421).

## PART B — Corporate side: the heavy evidence

### Degree/credential inflation — the PRIMARY reports
- **Fuller & Raman (2017), *Dismissed by Degrees* (HBS / Accenture / Grads of
  Life).** [EVIDENCE — 26M postings + ~600 exec survey]
  https://www.hbs.edu/managing-the-future-of-work/Documents/dismissed-by-degrees.pdf
  Signature stat: **~67% of production-supervisor postings demand a bachelor's while
  only ~16% of incumbents hold one**; 6M+ jobs at risk of inflation. → **Canonical
  primary evidence** that the bar is set *above* what the job needs — the exact
  "better gear than the raid drops" shape. (Replaces the AEI secondary summary as
  the anchor.)
- **Fuller et al. (2022), *The Emerging Degree Reset* (Burning Glass Institute /
  HBS).** https://www.burningglassinstitute.org/research/the-emerging-degree-reset
  — 46% of middle-skill / 31% of high-skill roles saw "degree resets" 2017–2019. →
  The bar is a **reversible policy choice**, not an objective requirement (also a
  complication — see below).

### Sociology of credentialism & signaling — the theory bedrock
- **Collins (1979), *The Credential Society*, Academic Press (reissued Columbia UP
  2019).** [THEORY] https://cup.columbia.edu/book/the-credential-society/9780231192354/
  Credential inflation is a self-reinforcing spiral; credentials are **occupational
  closure/stratification**, not skill certification. → Theoretical bedrock for
  treating requirements as gatekeeping detached from job content.
- **Spence (1973), "Job Market Signaling," *QJE* 87(3):355–374.** [EVIDENCE —
  foundational, Nobel-cited] https://competitionandappropriation.econ.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/95/2017/08/Spence1973.pdf
  Education can carry labor-market value as a **signal** independent of productivity
  added. → Explains *why* a credential persists as a filter even when it teaches
  nothing job-relevant. **This is the corporate analog of GearScore-as-signal.**
- **Caplan (2018), *The Case Against Education*, Princeton UP.** [OPINION — uses
  data] https://www.econlib.org/schooling-is-mostly-signaling/ — estimates ~80% of
  education's payoff is signaling (intelligence/conscientiousness/conformity). The
  80% figure is **contested** (see complications).
- **Labaree (1997), *How to Succeed in School Without Really Learning*, Yale UP.**
  https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300078671/ — credentials as **positional
  goods**: their value erodes as they spread, *forcing escalation*. → The mechanism
  behind the spiral (mirrors GearScore inflation as everyone stacks the number).

### The asymmetry — IC gauntlet vs. exec network hiring
- **Kim & Low, "Who Hires Whom? Connected Hiring in the CEO Labor Market," *J.
  Empirical Finance* 87 (2026)** *(confirm author first names/pagination).*
  [EVIDENCE — peer-reviewed] https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0927539826000113
  **37% of external CEOs socially connected to the hiring board** vs. **~5% by
  chance**; connected candidates **~35% more likely to be appointed.** → Hard anchor
  for the "hired over dinner" top-of-org asymmetry, quantified.
- **McPherson, Smith-Lovin & Cook (2001), "Birds of a Feather: Homophily in Social
  Networks," *Annual Review of Sociology* 27:415–444.** DOI 10.1146/annurev.soc.27.1.415.
  [EVIDENCE — review] → *Why* network hiring reproduces sameness; the mechanism
  behind old-boys'-network + cultural matching.
- **interviewing.io, "How well do LeetCode ratings predict interview performance?"**
  [EVIDENCE — proprietary data; verify coefficients on live page]
  https://interviewing.io/blog/how-well-do-leetcode-ratings-predict-interview-performance
  Problems-solved vs. FAANG offer correlation ≈ **0.17** (weak); contest rating ≈
  **no correlation.** → The grueling IC screen is a **weak signal** — rigor without
  validity, sharpening "asymmetry + arbitrariness."
- **Will Larson (lethain.com), "Interviewing engineering executives."** [OPINION —
  practitioner] https://lethain.com/interviewing-eng-executives/ — top exec roles
  "often never reach recruiters at all," running through investor/relationship
  networks. → Qualitative counterpart to the IC gauntlet.

### The driver — risk aversion, blame diffusion, cultural matching
- **Rivera (2015), *Pedigree: How Elite Students Get Elite Jobs*, Princeton UP.**
  [EVIDENCE — qual sociology inside elite firms] https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691155623/pedigree
  Hiring via **"cultural matching"/"fit"** (shared leisure, class-coded signals),
  often over grades — filtering out qualified lower-income candidates. → The
  definitive account of hiring as **similarity-seeking / self-image protection**,
  not objective competence.
- **Baert (2018), "Hiring a Gay Man, Taking a Risk?" *J. Homosexuality* 65(8)**
  *(confirm authorship of final version).* [EVIDENCE — lab experiment]
  https://www.iza.org/publications/dp/9536 — favorable-hire likelihood **drops
  ~31.7% per SD increase in employer risk aversion.** → Rare causal evidence:
  **risk aversion → safe, conformist hiring.**
- **"Nobody ever got fired for buying IBM"** — blame-diffusion aphorism (Forbes,
  Sutherland). [OPINION — framing device only] https://www.forbes.com/sites/duenablomstrom1/2018/11/30/nobody-gets-fired-for-buying-ibm-but-they-should/
  → Memorable framing for **credential-as-liability-shield.** Not data.

### The entry-level catch-22
- Posting analyses (journalism/vendor): ~35–38% of "entry-level" postings require
  3+ years; rationale is **shrinking the applicant pile**, not job need. GCFGlobal
  https://edu.gcfglobal.org/en/jobsearchandnetworking/the-entrylevel-catch-22/1/ ;
  Congress.net https://congress.net/entry-level-jobs-increasingly-demand-years-of-experience-that-new-workers-simply-cannot-have/
  → Quantifies "need experience to get experience," tied to gatekeeper convenience.
  **Caveat:** strongest numbers are journalism/vendor, **not peer-reviewed.** No
  clean academic at-scale quantification found — lean on Collins + *Dismissed by
  Degrees* for the mechanism.

## PART C — CONTRADICTS / COMPLICATES (take seriously)

### On the WoW side
1. **"Poor proxy" → soften to "noisy, *biased* proxy."** Theorycraft communities
   produce **genuine** competence (**Steinkuehler & Duncan 2008**, "Scientific
   Habits of Mind in Virtual Worlds," *J. Sci. Educ. Tech.* 17(6),
   https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10956-008-9120-8 — ~86% "social
   knowledge construction," >50% systems reasoning), and high scorers are drawn from
   the analytic power-gamer cohort. So gear/score and skill are **positively
   correlated via selection**, even though the metric doesn't *measure* play. The
   honest claim is "**gameable, biased proxy**," not "meaningless."
2. **Gating is rational triage under anonymity.** Tobold
   (http://tobolds.blogspot.com/2010/05/i-dont-think-gearscore-is-culprit-here.html):
   PUG discrimination is driven by **anonymity and risk, not the addon** — remove
   the metric and leaders find another proxy. Spinks
   (https://spinksville.wordpress.com/2010/01/06/gearscore-and-why-we-need-to-evaluate-other-players/):
   with strangers, gear/experience is "pretty much all you have to go on." Wowpedia
   concedes score "can provide a basic indication… of readiness" against real
   wipe/repair costs. → The gate is **social**, the number just makes it efficient;
   the convenience is *real*, not imaginary.
3. **Anti-elitist communities succeed anyway.** Snodgrass et al. (2017), casual-
   raiding guilds, *New Media & Society* — gating is a *choice*, not a necessity.

### On the corporate side
4. **Structured multi-round interviews have real validity.** Schmidt & Hunter
   (1998), *Psychological Bulletin* 124(2) — **structured interviews r≈.51** vs.
   unstructured .38; work samples high. → The critique targets *arbitrary/
   unstructured* screens (LeetCode puzzles), **not** multi-step assessment per se.
   (Caveat: some 1998 validities revised downward by Sackett et al. 2022.)
5. **Degree requirements are partly *declining*** (*Emerging Degree Reset*, 2022).
   Complicates "bars only ratchet up."
6. **…but the reset is mostly rhetoric.** *Skills-Based Hiring: The Long Road…*
   (Burning Glass/HBS, 2024) https://www.burningglassinstitute.org/research/skills-based-hiring-2024
   — dropping degree requirements changed **fewer than 1 in 700 hires.** → Cuts both
   ways: reset unrealized *and* gatekeeping is behaviorally sticky (reinforces core
   thesis).
7. **Signaling estimates may be overstated** — critiques of Caplan argue he
   conflates signaling with ability bias. Credentials plausibly carry *some* real
   human-capital signal; "pure gatekeeping, zero skill" is the contestable strong
   version.
8. **Network hiring can carry information, not just cronyism.** The connected-hiring
   finding is consistent *both* with old-boys'-network favoritism *and* with
   efficient private-information transfer about a candidate's true ability. Don't
   present 37% as unambiguous nepotism.

## What this changes in the case

- **Anchor the corporate side on primary sources:** *Dismissed by Degrees* (67% vs.
  16%), Spence signaling, Collins closure, the Kim & Low 37% connected-hiring stat,
  Rivera cultural matching, Baert risk-aversion→conformist hiring.
- **Add the strongest conceptual bridge:** Consalvo's **"gaming capital"** ↔
  corporate **credential/signal** — GearScore and a diploma do the same social work.
- **Correct our own claim:** GearScore/RaiderIO is a **noisy, biased, gameable
  proxy** (correlated with skill via selection), *not* a "meaningless" one. Update
  `synthesis.md` accordingly.
- **Steelman honestly:** concede gating is partly rational triage under anonymity/
  risk, and that structured assessment has real validity — the target is *inflation
  past function* and *asymmetry*, and the ethical core is **cost redistribution onto
  those who can't bootstrap in**, not pure irrationality.

## Intended reader

General reader, possibly non-gamer. Every WoW concept gets a one-line explanation
on first use. The hook is: the workplace lessons are real; the guild is just where
they show up fastest and clearest.

## Proposed structure

1. **Hook** — a vivid raid-night moment that any manager would recognize.
2. **The claim** — a guild is a voluntary organization running the same
   coordination problems as a company, only faster and with the exits wide open.
3. **Why the guild is a better lab** — voluntary participation + fast feedback +
   transparent performance data (logs) surface dynamics the office hides.
4. **The cases** — the strongest parallels, one section each (populated from the
   knowledge base). Likely grouped by the themes in `../knowledge-base/themes.md`.
5. **Where the analogy breaks** — the honest section: pay, livelihood, exit cost.
   This is what keeps the piece credible.
6. **So what** — what a leader can actually take from the guild to the office.
7. **Close** — return to the opening raid-night moment.
